# The Dawn of Autonomy

In the early 2020s, developers relied on autocomplete systems. Today, we have moved from autocomplete to **autonomy**. Agents can now plan architecture, create UI layouts, and deploy backend schemas entirely on their own.

## Key Takeaways
- **Efficiency:** 10x faster development cycles.
- **Cost:** Near-zero marginal cost for content creation.
- **Quality:** Self-reviewing loops eliminate basic errors.

This shift allows human engineers to focus on product strategy and user experience rather than boilerplate code.
